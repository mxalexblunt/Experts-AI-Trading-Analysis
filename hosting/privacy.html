Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Experts handles information when you use the app and the support pages hosted at expertsanalysisapp.web.app. It is a practical template for transparency and is not legal advice.

What Experts does

Experts is an educational market-analysis app for US stocks and ETFs. It helps users review market context and generate AI-assisted reports from multiple perspectives. Experts does not provide financial advice.

Information stored on your device

The app stores watchlist items, recent searches, and AI consent settings locally on your device using local app storage. This local app data is not uploaded by Experts as a user account profile.

Information sent to third-party services

Experts uses Finnhub and Twelve Data to retrieve ticker search results, quotes, company information, news, and chart history. The app may send the ticker or asset you request to those providers so it can display market data.

If you grant AI data consent and generate a report, Experts uses Google Gemini to create educational market analysis. The request may include the ticker, market data, news summaries, optional notes you enter, and any chart screenshot you choose to attach.

Photos and camera access

Experts may ask for camera or photo library access only when you choose to add a chart screenshot for AI analysis. The selected image is used as optional analysis context. Experts does not provide a public photo gallery or social posting feature.

Support requests

If you submit the Support Form, the hosted page stores your request in Firebase Realtime Database. A support request may include your selected topic, message, optional contact information, page URL, referrer, browser user agent, status, and submission time.

Payments, accounts, and location

The current app does not include subscriptions, ads, payment features, user accounts, public profiles, messaging, or location-based features.

Children

Experts is not intended for children under 13. Do not submit personal information through the support form if you are under 13.

Your choices

  • You can revoke AI data consent in the app Settings screen.
  • You can avoid attaching chart screenshots to AI requests.
  • You can remove local app data by deleting the app or clearing app data through your device controls.
  • You can use the Support Form for privacy or data questions.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Experts changes. The latest version will be posted on this page.

Contact

Use the Support Form for privacy questions, support requests, or feedback.
